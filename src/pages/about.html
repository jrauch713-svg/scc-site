Hey, I'm Josh.

I'm new to running a business. What I'm not new to is solving other people's problems. Let me solve your trash, junk or general clutter issues in a way that leaves you satisfied, and able to tackle whatever else weighs on your day.

Why I say "we appreciate your business"

Not long ago I was having a rough day. My phone was dead, I couldn't get to my bank, and I probably looked like I had nowhere to go. I tried three places. The smallest one, a family-owned food store, was the only one that helped without treating me like a bother.

On my way out I saw their sign: "We appreciate your business." Plenty of places say it. That store meant it. They bridged the gap between platitude and attitude.

That's how I want to run this. No matter how big or small your job is, or what kind of day you're having, we appreciate your business, and we'll show it, so you know it.

How we work

Local. We work anywhere in Houston and The Woodlands, at hours that fit your schedule.

Upfront. A free estimate from your photo, then a firm quote in person before anything gets lifted.

Not wasteful. Usable things get resold or rehomed first. Only real junk goes to the dump.
