Items with extra fees

Some things cost more to get rid of properly. For these, you pay what the landfill or recycler charges us, at cost, plus one flat handling fee. Both show up on your quote.

Questions and answers

How much does junk removal cost?

Haul-away is priced by how much space your stuff takes up: a few items $99–$149, a small load $175–$275, a half load $325–$450, a full load $550–$750. Each price includes the loading, the hauling and the regular dump fee. Items with facility fees cost extra.

Is the estimate free, and is it the final price?

The estimate is free. Text or email a photo and your ZIP code, and we send back a ballpark price. Once we see the job in person, you get a firm quote. Nothing gets lifted until you say yes.

Do you take just one or two items?

Yes. A few items, like a couch, a chair or an old dresser, run $99–$149. Small and odd jobs are welcome.

Which items cost extra?

Fridges, freezers, window AC units and dehumidifiers; mattresses and box springs; TVs and computer monitors; and concrete, dirt, bricks and roofing. You pay the facility fee at cost, plus $15 each for handling, and both show up on your quote.

Can you take paint, chemicals, propane tanks, tires or car batteries?

These need special handling. Send a photo and ask before the job, and we'll make sure they end up in the right place.

Do I pay a deposit?

A deposit books haul-aways and comes off your final bill.

Can you just do the lifting?

Yes. Muscle-only help is $40 an hour with a 2-hour minimum, for loading a truck or dumpster you have, or moving heavy things.

What happens to the things you pick up?

Usable items are resold or rehomed rather than dumped.

Where do you work?

Across Greater Houston: Waller, Sugar Land, Pearland, Crosby, The Woodlands and everywhere in between.

When can you come?

Our hours are flexible and we work around your schedule. Tell us your deadline when you send the photo.
