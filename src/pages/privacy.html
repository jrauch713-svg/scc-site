Privacy

Short version: this website doesn't use cookies or tracking, and what you send us is used only to quote and do your job.

Last updated November 6, 2026.

This website

The site has no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking scripts. We don't keep visitor logs.

Two outside services deliver the site to your browser and see your IP address while doing it, the way any website host does: Amazon Web Services (CloudFront) serves the pages, and Google Fonts serves the typeface.

What you send us

When you text, call or email us, we get what you choose to share: your phone number or email, photos of the items, your ZIP code or address, and details about the job.

We use it to give you an estimate, schedule and do the job, and follow up about that job. We don't sell it, rent it, or share it for marketing.

Texts and calls go through our phone service provider, and email goes through Google. They handle your messages under their own privacy policies.

Photos

We may ask to take before and after photos of a job. We only show them on the site or our listings with your OK, and never with faces, house numbers or anything else that identifies you.

Your choices

Ask us to delete your messages and photos at any time, and we will, unless we need a record of a completed job for taxes or a dispute.
