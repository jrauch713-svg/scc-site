Our tools

Scout, our listing assistant

Scout is an internal tool that helps our small team notice free items people are giving away near Houston, so good things get picked up instead of thrown out. It suggests which posts are worth a trip. A person always decides and writes every message.

  • Reads only public posts, through each site's official tools and API. No scraping.
  • Never posts, replies or messages anyone on its own.
  • Follows each group's rules, and we always tell givers when an item may be resold.
  • Stores no personal data about posters beyond what's needed to decide on a pickup.
